The model

How it’s meant to scale
01

A living catalog

Music isn’t shipped on a schedule — it’s produced continuously and set free in volume. The catalog is meant to keep growing rather than sit still — released freely as the work is made.

02

Publishing & rights

Every track is named, organized, and catalogued — a publishing system built for long-term ownership and licensing, not a pile of loose files.

03

Made to travel

One channel today, every channel over time. The work is built to reach anyone, anywhere, and to keep reaching them long after it’s made.

The aim was never a single hit — it’s a body of work large enough to outlast any one song.

Licensing & sync

The catalog is open for licensing — film, games, brands, creators, and anyone who wants a sound that doesn’t exist anywhere else.

The horizon

IAMAIC begins as one human and many machines making music together. It’s built to become something larger.

A music and media company where the making, the publishing, and the distribution keep running — and the catalog keeps growing far past today’s 2,000. The human moves from creator to operator to steward; the machines keep singing.

The next chapter reaches past the catalog. A team built to teach the craft — so anyone with the will to learn can compose, produce, and build a life in music with AI beside them. Talent was never the only way in. Consistency was always enough — and now, for the first time, it’s within anyone’s reach.
